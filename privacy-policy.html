Scam Check Malaysia (“ScamCheck”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the Scam Check Android application and related website (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

a) Public verification checks
When you check a website, phone number, bank account, company, investment name, or product name, that input is used only to produce the verification result for that request. We do not keep public checks as searchable user history.

b) Community reports (optional)
If you submit a community report, you provide report details and consent. Submitted reports may be stored for moderation and public-interest display. Reporting is separate from a normal check.

c) Technical information
The App may process limited technical data needed to operate the service (for example basic request handling, rate limiting, or stability diagnostics if enabled by the platform). We do not sell personal data.

d) What we do not require for public checks
Public checks do not require creating an account. We do not intentionally collect government ID numbers for advertising profiles.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide verification results against public/official source lists and curated references
  • Moderate community reports when submitted with consent
  • Improve reliability, security, and abuse prevention (including rate limits)
  • Respond to support requests

3. Data Storage

  • Public check inputs are processed to return a result and are not retained as searchable user history
  • Community reports are stored only when explicitly submitted with consent
  • Admin configuration and operational data may be stored on our servers (for example Firebase / Google Cloud) to run the service

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. Data may be disclosed only:

  • To infrastructure providers needed to operate the App (for example Google Play / Firebase / hosting)
  • When required by law
